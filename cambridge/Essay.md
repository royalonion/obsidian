In our changing world, politics is gaining more attention than before, reshaping our world. One of the most debated topics is the future: how is our government planning to tackle it?

On the one hand, there is a core issue with the way our diet is built up, which would be a key to a healthy life. Children usually receive education about consuming enough vegetables in their preschools and elementary schools. However, at this age, their eating habits are mostly influenced by their parents, who are likely to rely on cheaper or faster alternatives to save money and time. Another aspect is that while teens today may receive training, this was not always the case: their elders could be uneducated about the downsides of fast food.

On the other hand, our world is rapidly heating up, due to the well-known effects of greenhouse gases and the lack of caring about reusable energy sources. While solar plants and wind turbines are on a rise, they are still on the lower end of the scale, compared to, for example, coal-based power plants. This leads to an increase in global warming, and while companies are not heavily affected by the rising heat, we, people are. Everybody knows that our summers are getting hotter and it is not going to get any better.
