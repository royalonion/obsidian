# part 1
Are you a picky eater? Do you prefer your local cuisine rather than buying fast food? If yes, there are some solid reasons behind your decisions, which may feel unintentional at times.

On the one hand, your childhood experiences affects your food palette greater than you would believe. The human brain associates sugar with safety, therefore any sweets, fruits or desserts you regularly have in your first years usually becomes your favourite delicacies.
In contrast to this, sour and savoury food is usually linked to poisonous, or unripe fruits, therefore, if you rarely eat them, you may grow a sense of dislike or even disgust towards those.
That being said, if you eat savoury foods regularly as a child, you are more likely to turn up enjoying them.

On the other hand, the availability of certain foods also plays a leading role in the development of your taste. If your meals are cooked from locally sourced food, for example from vegetables that you have bought at a local market, the food could seem more healthy, therefore better. In contrast to this, if you follow exotic recipes, you could end up with a stew that is amazing by itself, but rather unappealing to you, as your body is not familiar with the rare, new added spices.

So what affects your mind when choosing what to eat? Well, it is a combination of both, but your childhood experience is greater when it comes to setting your preference. What your grandmother once made you could easily become your comfort meal.

## Proposal on raising awareness of environmental issues
### Introduction
The aim of this proposal is to outline ideas for raising awareness of the major environmental issues in our area. Many students are facing the direct effects of littering and the lack of gardening in areas on campus.
### Community service
A simple, straightforward solution is to ask help from the locals living in the problematic area. If a community service program would be established, everybody could help in keeping the area clean. Not only could this reduce the amount of money paid by the local council to cleaning companies, but it could also help build a strong community in the neighbourhood. To make the program more attractive, some activists could prepare food for the hard-working citizens.
### Automatic equipment
The installation of automatic gardening equipment could greatly improve the current state of public lawns and gardens. An irrigation system would help keep the grass green even in the hottest summer days, freeing up the exhausted gardeners, who could instead keep the flowers tidy. Moreover, the implementation of such a system could save huge amounts of money in water bills.
Another viable option would involve the installation of a robotic lawnmower, which, if set up correctly, could keep the grass fertilised, and healthy, by keeping an ideal height, while emptying the cut-off parts at the roots.

### Conclusion
All things considered, the implementation of the listed techniques would greatly benefit not only the area, but also its citizens. I strongly recommend implementing automated devices, and urge the college to take action as soon as possible.
