# part 1
Are you a picky eater? Do you prefer your local cuisine rather than buying fast food? If yes, there are some solid reasons behind your decisions, which may feel unintentional at times.

On the one hand, your childhood experiences affects your food palette greater than you would believe. The human brain associates sugar with safety, therefore any sweets, fruits or desserts you regularly have in your first years usually becomes your favourite delicacies.
In contrast to this, sour and savoury food is usually linked to poisonous, or unripe fruits, therefore, if you rarely eat them, you may grow a sense of dislike or even disgust towards those.
That being said, if you eat savoury foods regularly as a child, you are more likely to turn up enjoying them.

On the other hand, the availability of certain foods also plays a leading role in the development of your taste. If your meals are cooked from locally sourced food, for example from vegetables that you have bought at a local market, the food could seem more healthy, therefore better. In contrast to this, if you follow exotic recipes, you could end up with a stew that is amazing by itself, but rather unappealing to you, as your body is not familiar with the rare, new added spices.

So what affects your mind when choosing what to eat? Well, it is a combination of both, but your childhood experience is greater when it comes to setting your preference. What your grandmother once made you could easily become your comfort meal.
